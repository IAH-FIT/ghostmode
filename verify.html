What stays outside our control

In transit
Your request reaches this machine through our website host and a network provider, encrypted. They see connection details, as every website's host does. Neither sees a question in the clear on our side, and neither keeps one.
Your browser
It keeps one yes flag once you pass the adults-only notice, and whatever you choose to save. The microphone uses your browser's own speech service; type when it must stay fully private.

On your side

We keep nothing. The rest is on your device, your browser and your network, and those are yours to set. Five habits cover it.

Private window
Open Ghost Mode in a private or incognito window. When you close it, the browser forgets the page, the password and the yes flag.
Type it
The microphone uses your browser's own speech service. Typing goes only to us.
Your own network
A work or school network sees which sites you reach, so use your own connection or your phone's.
Your own device
On a managed or shared device, the owner can see the screen. Use a device only you control.
Save, then close
The report exists only on your screen. Save it where you choose, then close the tab, and it is gone from everywhere else.
